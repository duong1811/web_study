Why we exist

A good teacher can make the world feel bigger.

Work and life keep changing. The chance to learn something new should not depend on where you live, what your calendar looks like, or whether you already know all the right words.

So we brought thoughtful teachers, practical projects, and genuinely encouraging people together in one place. Every course is built to help you move from “I wonder if I could” to “look what I made.”

That's Northstar: a welcoming little nudge toward whatever comes next.
