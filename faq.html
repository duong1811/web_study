01 · Your first step

Getting started

How do I choose the right course?+

Start with the skill you'd like to build. Each course page shows the level, time commitment, outcomes, and curriculum so you can pick what fits. Still unsure? Ask our team.

Do I need any experience to begin?+

Not at all. Courses are clearly marked by level, and our beginner-friendly courses start with the essentials. You can learn wherever you are today.

How much time should I set aside?+

Most learners spend two to three hours a week. Lessons are short and self-paced, so you can fit them around your life.

02 · Learning that lasts

Courses & certificates

How long do I have access to a course?+

Course access is yours for life. Learn at your pace and come back whenever you want a refresher or a little inspiration.

Will I earn a certificate?+

Yes. Complete the course lessons and project to earn a shareable certificate of completion.

Can I learn on my phone or tablet?+

Yes. The learning experience works across modern phones, tablets, and desktop browsers. Some hands-on projects may be easier on a computer.
